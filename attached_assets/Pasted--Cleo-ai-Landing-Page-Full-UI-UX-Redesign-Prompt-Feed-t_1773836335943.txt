# Cleo.ai Landing Page — Full UI/UX Redesign Prompt

> Feed this prompt directly into an AI design tool (v0, Lovable, Builder.io, Bolt, or a Figma AI plugin) to generate a fully redesigned landing page.

---

## CONTEXT & BRAND READING

**Product**: Cleo.ai — an AI-powered professional networking and matchmaking platform connecting founders, investors, and talent.

**Current page analysis**:
- Dark navy/indigo background (#0D0B1E range) with purple (#7C3AED) as the primary accent
- Hero headline: "Meet the right people. Faster." — clear, punchy, good
- Chat widget mockup on the right showing an AI conversation flow with match results
- Two CTAs: "Get Started →" (filled) and "Log In" (ghost border)
- Social proof bar at bottom: "Trusted by founders, investors, and talent worldwide"
- Tagline: "Cleo is your AI Superconnector — matching founders, investors, talent, and partners through intelligent conversations."

**Core gap**: The design is competent but generic — it looks like dozens of AI SaaS dark-mode landing pages. It lacks emotional resonance, trust signals, specificity, and a scroll journey. The chat mockup is the most compelling element but is underutilized. There is no social proof with faces/names, no outcome metrics, and no clear differentiation from LinkedIn, Lunchclub, or Bumble Bizz.

---

## REDESIGN BRIEF

### Aesthetic Direction
**"Precision Intelligence" — Dark luxury meets editorial precision.**

Think: the visual weight of Notion combined with the depth of Linear, but with the human warmth of a curated members club. Not a chatbot product — a concierge. The purple accent should evolve from a neon SaaS color into a rich, jewel-toned violet (#6D28D9 → #5B21B6). Introduce a warm off-white (#F5F0EB) for contrast sections. Use a single editorial serif display font (e.g., Playfair Display, Canela, or Editorial New) for the hero headline — only the hero — paired with a clean geometric sans (e.g., DM Sans, Syne, or Neue Montreal) for body and UI elements.

**NOT**: Generic purple gradient + white SaaS look. No floating blobs. No gradient mesh backgrounds. No Inter font.

## SECTION-BY-SECTION REDESIGN INSTRUCTIONS

### 1. NAVIGATION BAR
- Keep it minimal: Logo left, "Log In" ghost button + "Get Started →" filled button on right
- Add a subtle frosted glass effect on scroll (backdrop-filter: blur(12px)) with a very thin 1px border at bottom
- Logo: Replace the current purple square "C" with a wordmark + a small node/connection glyph (two dots connected by a line) in violet
- Nav background: transparent until user scrolls 80px, then transitions to semi-opaque dark

### 2. HERO SECTION
**Layout**: Split — left 55% text + CTA, right 45% chat mockup (kept, but redesigned)

**Headline**:
```
[Serif display font, large — ~72px desktop]
Meet the right people.
[Violet accent word, slightly italic] Faster.
```
Keep the current headline — it works. But render "Faster." in the editorial serif, slightly italicized, and in the jewel violet. The rest in the same serif, white. Give it generous line-height (1.1).

**Sub-headline** (keep current copy but improve styling):
```
Cleo is your AI Superconnector — matching founders, investors, 
talent, and partners through intelligent conversations.
```
Render in DM Sans, 18px, #A09FB5 (muted lavender-grey). Max-width 420px.

**CTAs**:
- Primary: "Get Started →" — filled violet (#6D28D9), rounded-lg (10px), 52px height, DM Sans 500 weight, subtle glow shadow on hover
- Secondary: "See How It Works" — no border, just text with an underline animation on hover, arrow icon. Remove "Log In" from hero — it's a distraction at this stage.

**Pill badge above headline**:
Replace the current green dot + "AI-Powered Networking" with:
`✦ Members-only · Invite or apply`
Style: thin border, frosted glass pill, smaller font (11px uppercase tracking-widest), violet dot

**Right side — Chat Mockup Redesign**:
- Upgrade to a floating phone/browser frame with a soft violet outer glow (box-shadow: 0 0 60px rgba(109,40,217,0.3))
- Show 3 match cards — not 2. Add a third with a different sector (e.g., "Priya Sharma — Angel Investor · Consumer · 94% match")
- Add a small animated typing indicator (the "..." dots) that pulses
- Show a match percentage bar under each card — a thin violet progress bar at ~90%
- The chat bubbles: user message bubble should be a more premium violet (#7C3AED), not flat purple. AI bubbles should be #1E1B2E (darker card).
- Add a micro-animation: the cards appear one by one with a 0.2s stagger on page load

**Background treatment**:
- Behind the chat mockup: a very subtle radial gradient bloom in violet (opacity 15%) on the dark background — creates depth without being cliché
- Add a very faint diagonal grid or dot-matrix pattern (opacity 4%) to the full hero background to add texture without distraction

## ANIMATION & INTERACTION SPECS

- **Page load**: Hero headline fades + slides up (transform: translateY(20px) → 0, opacity 0 → 1, duration 0.6s ease-out, stagger 0.1s per line)
- **Chat mockup**: Match cards appear one by one, staggered 0.2s each, slide in from right
- **Typing indicator**: 3-dot pulse animation (CSS keyframes, infinite loop, 0.4s between each dot)
- **CTA hover**: Slight scale(1.02) + deeper glow shadow
- **Metrics counter**: Count-up animation on scroll-into-view (IntersectionObserver)
- **Persona columns**: On hover, slight violet background wash + border glow transition (0.2s ease)
- **Nav**: Smooth transition from transparent to frosted-glass on scroll

---

## RESPONSIVENESS

- **Mobile** (≤768px): Stack hero vertically. Chat mockup moves below text. 3-step flow becomes vertical accordion. Persona columns stack. Metrics become 2×2 grid.
- **Tablet** (768–1024px): 2-column grid where possible. Chat mockup scales to 80%.
- Touch targets: minimum 44×44px for all interactive elements.

---

## TECHNICAL STACK RECOMMENDATION

- **Framework**: Next.js 14 (App Router) or plain HTML/Tailwind CSS for static
- **Animations**: Framer Motion (for React) or CSS keyframes
- **Fonts**: Google Fonts — Playfair Display + DM Sans (or self-host via Fontsource)
- **Icons**: Lucide React or custom SVG
- **Chat mockup**: Pure CSS/HTML component (no external dependencies)

---

## WHAT TO EXPLICITLY AVOID

- ❌ Purple gradient blobs or mesh backgrounds
- ❌ Inter or Roboto as the primary font
- ❌ Cookie-cutter AI SaaS layout (full-width banner headline, centered text, generic screenshot)
- ❌ Generic stock illustration or Lottie animations that feel off-brand
- ❌ More than 2 CTAs visible at any one time
- ❌ Floating "chat with us" widget that competes with the product mockup
- ❌ Social proof without names, numbers, or specifics
