## 6. Tone & voice cheat sheet

- Confident, not loud. We're the quiet relevance engine, not the megaphone.
- India-native. Use "ecosystem", "founder", "operator", "warm intro" — skip Silicon Valley clichés.
- Show, don't pitch. Posts should feel like a smart friend explaining a thing, not a billboard.
- No emoji spam. One per post, max — and only if it earns its place.
- Always end with a soft CTA: `cleya.ai` or "join the waitlist".
